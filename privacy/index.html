Sweat & Glory: Pixel Walk RPG Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026
Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the iOS game Sweat & Glory: Pixel Walk RPG (Chinese title 《汗水荣光》, "Sweat & Glory" or "the Game"). The Game is developed and provided by Mao Tianxiang (茆添翔), an independent developer based in mainland China ("we", "us").

1. Summary

  • We (the developer) do not collect your data and cannot access it. The Game has no developer servers, no account system, no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising tools, and does not track you. Your game data is stored on your device; if you turn on iCloud backup, it is also stored in your own iCloud account; and in face-to-face battles or when you share a memento, some information goes directly to the other person (see Sections 4, 5 and 6). We receive your email address and message only if you choose to email us (see Section 7).
  • Health data is read-only and used only on your device. Data the Game reads from Apple Health is used only on your device to calculate in-game growth and progress and to show in-game displays (see Section 2.1 for how each type is used). It is never uploaded to us, never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with any third party.
  • iCloud backup is off by default. If you turn it on, backups are stored in your own iCloud account, which we cannot access. Backups contain game progress only, not records read from Health (see Section 4).
  • Face-to-face battles connect devices directly. While the Battle tab is in the foreground, nearby devices can see a small amount of information, such as your player name and class (see Section 5).
  • No ads. The current version has no in-app purchases.
  • Sweat & Glory is an entertainment product. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice or diagnosis. In-game numbers are for gameplay only.

2. Data the Game reads and uses on your device

2.1 Health data (Apple Health / HealthKit, read-only, with your permission)

With your permission, the Game reads these types:

  • Steps
  • Walking + running distance
  • Active energy
  • Sleep analysis (in bed, asleep, deep, light/core, REM)
  • Workouts: only running, swimming and cycling, used to calculate exercise minutes and cardio minutes

Purpose: Only for gameplay and in-game displays on your device. Each type is used as follows:

  • Steps: converted day by day into your character's permanent HP (with a daily cap) and XP (for example, reaching 10,000 steps in a day earns 100 XP, and each consecutive day you reach the goal adds +2%, up to ×1.6). The honor (shop currency) you earn each day is also calculated from steps. Steps are also used for step medals, class-change requirements, class affinity, HP estimates in practice battles, and step counts shown on the home screen, in battle reports and in daily reminders.
  • Sleep: sleeping 7 hours or more gives that day's XP a small bonus. Sleep is also used for sleep and early-riser medals, the Cleric's and Adventurer's class-change requirements, the Cleric's daily XP multiplier, class affinity, and sleep displays. Sleep does not affect battle stats.
  • Workouts: converted into aerobic minutes, used for the Ranger, Rogue and Adventurer class-change requirements, class affinity, and displays on the Health data screen and in battle reports. Workouts do not affect HP or XP.
  • Distance and active energy: shown on the Health data screen only; they do not affect gameplay.

Class-change requirements and class affinity use the last 7 days of data. Steps and workouts added manually in the Health app are not counted. If a night has only manually added sleep, or sleep without sleep stages, that night's sleep score is capped at a lower level and it does not earn the 7-hour XP bonus.

We do not: upload health data to any server; use health data for advertising, marketing or data mining; sell health data; disclose health data to third parties; or write any data to Health (the Game only requests read access).

2.2 Motion & Fitness (device pedometer)

If Health has no steps for the day, the Game fills in with your device's step data through iOS Motion & Fitness (the two are never added together). These steps are used in the same way as steps from Health. Raw pedometer data is not stored separately; only the HP, XP, honor and medals calculated from it are saved. It is processed only on your device and never uploaded. Only the pedometer triggers the Motion & Fitness permission; the Game does not use iOS motion activity detection.

Separately, character artwork shifts slightly as you tilt your phone (a visual effect that needs no permission). For this, the Game reads your device's orientation in real time. It is used only on your device at that moment and is never stored, uploaded or advertised, and it does not affect any game values. The effect is turned off when Reduce Motion is on. Battles use touch controls only; there are no motion or gesture controls.

2.3 Profile data you create in the Game

This includes your age, gender, class, character appearance, custom player name, game progress, battle history, medals, shop items and game settings. This data is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud backup, in your own iCloud account (see Section 4). Age is used for in-game number conversion and class-related calculations (for example, step rules for players under 18; see Section 9). We cannot access this data.

2.4 Game Center

The Game can use Apple Game Center sign-in, but you can play fully without signing in; if you don't, your player name appears as "Player" followed by 4 random digits. Apple handles Game Center sign-in. The Game only reads your Game Center nickname on your device to use as your player name. It does not read your Game Center friends list, and the current version does not use Game Center leaderboards. Apple's handling of Game Center data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

2.5 Camera

The camera is used only to scan memento QR codes. Recognition happens in real time on your device, and the Game does not send camera images to us.

2.6 Local network and Bluetooth

Used only to find and connect to nearby devices for face-to-face battles (see Section 5). The first time you open the Battle tab, iOS asks whether to allow local network access. iOS may also ask for Bluetooth permission: if you allow it, the system can use Bluetooth to find and connect to nearby players. The Game itself does not use Bluetooth to read data from other devices or accessories. The local network is not used to advertise your device name, but people nearby can see your player name (see Section 5).

2.7 Notifications

The Game only uses local notifications scheduled on your device (such as daily reports and reminders). There is no remote push server. You can turn them off in iOS Settings at any time.

2.8 Data the Game does not use

The Game does not use your location, contacts or advertising identifier, and the current version has no purchase history.

3. We do not collect, share or sell your data

Apple's App Privacy Details page defines "collect" as transmitting data off the device in a way that allows the developer and/or its third-party partners to access it for longer than is necessary to service the request in real time (https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/). Under this definition we do not collect your data, and the Game's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected":

  • We have no servers and no account system.
  • The Game contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking tools, so no third party receives your data through the Game.
  • We do not sell any data or use any data for advertising.

This does not mean that no data ever leaves your device. In the following cases data does leave your device, but neither we (the developer) nor any third-party partner can access it:

  • if you turn on iCloud backup, the backup is stored in your own iCloud account (see Section 4);
  • in face-to-face battles, information such as your player name goes directly to the nearby device of the other player, which keeps some match records (see Section 5);
  • mementos you choose to share are handled by the recipient or the sharing channel you use (see Section 6).

Health, Game Center, iCloud and face-to-face connections (MultipeerConnectivity) are system features provided by Apple and handled by Apple under its privacy policy. If you turn on "Share With App Developers" in iOS, Apple may provide developers with aggregated crash and usage statistics. Apple handles this; it is not data collected by the Game.

4. iCloud backup

  • iCloud backup uses the iCloud key-value store in your own iCloud account. We cannot access your iCloud account or the data in it.
  • Off by default. Backups are made only after you turn on "iCloud saves" in Profile > ⚙ Settings > Backup. You must be signed in to iCloud to turn it on.
  • What a backup contains: Each device keeps one backup, always labeled "iPhone"; your device name is not recorded. A backup includes:
    • character profile: your exact age (in whole years), gender, class, appearance, creation time, and class-change trial records (HP in trials is partly calculated from steps);
    • player name;
    • practice results and records of non-practice battles (including opponents' player names);
    • unlocked medals and the battle statistics related to them;
    • shop items;
    • settings, such as language, sound, vibration, daily battle report and reminder time, plus flags such as first launch;
    • Mementos in "Moments to Keep": the public part of each card (see Section 6), the player name on the card and where the card came from, without the encrypted save;
    • information used to tell backups apart and check them: a randomly generated device ID, a one-way hash of your iCloud account identifier, the save time and a checksum.
  • Growth is stored as running totals only. Backups do not include records read from Health, such as step, sleep or workout details. The HP, XP and honor calculated from that data are uploaded only as running totals, with no daily values. The dates of the last 7 days that were settled are kept, so it is still possible to see which of those days had a settlement, but their values are all zero.
  • Not uploaded: your highest single-day step count, and the dates you met your step goal, met your sleep goal or woke up early. So after you restore from a backup, your streak of goal days starts again from zero. Backups also do not include permission status.
  • Memento key: While "iCloud saves" is off, the key used to encrypt mementos is stored only in the iOS Keychain on this device and does not sync to other devices. It is written to your iCloud key-value store only after you turn on "iCloud saves".
  • Deleting: In Profile > ⚙ Settings > Backup > "Manage backups and restore", choose a backup and tap "Delete backup". Backups left by your other, older devices are not deleted automatically; you can delete them here too.

5. Face-to-face battles

Face-to-face battles use Apple's MultipeerConnectivity to connect nearby devices directly. Wi-Fi must be on; the system chooses Wi-Fi, peer-to-peer Wi-Fi or Bluetooth automatically. No internet connection is needed, and data does not pass through our servers.

  • Automatic advertising (visible to nearby devices, not encrypted): Your device advertises to nearby devices only while the Battle tab is in the foreground. People nearby can see your player name (used as the connection name; up to the first 12 characters). In lobby and nearby modes, the advertisement also includes the battle mode, a random seat ID and your class; when you create a room manually, only the connection name is shown. It does not include your level, honor, medals, device name or any health data.
  • After connecting (encrypted): The two devices exchange what is needed to start a battle: player name, class, gender, character appearance, battle stats (HP, attack, defense, crit, dodge, block), plus version, rule checksums, random values and match actions. Battle stats are numbers calculated from game progress, and HP is partly calculated from steps, but they do not contain the health records themselves, such as steps or sleep. Encryption is required for the session.
  • The connection does not verify the other player's identity. Only battle people you have confirmed in person.
  • What the other device keeps: After a non-practice battle, the other device keeps your player name, the match result, points earned, completion time and match ID, with no automatic deletion. If the other player has iCloud backup on, these records also sync to their iCloud. Your appearance, gender and battle stats are used only during the match and are not kept on the other device.

6. Mementos (QR sharing)

Mementos include the "Hero Card" created automatically after a win and the "Achievement Memento" you can create when you unlock a medal. Both are kept in "Moments to Keep".

Information leaves your device only when you choose to share or scan a memento. Once you share a memento, it is handled by the recipient or the sharing channel you use, which we cannot control.

  • Public part (readable by anyone who scans it): (1) card details: format version, card ID, card number, type (victory or achievement), creation time, for Achievement Mementos the related achievement, and a checksum for the whole card; (2) character: class, gender, appearance and level; (3) battle stats: HP, attack, defense, critical, dodge and block (HP is partly calculated from steps); (4) record: medals, matches played (practice and formal matches combined, including losses and draws) and your current win streak in formal battles (reset to zero by a loss or draw). The public part of the QR code does not contain your player name.
  • Shared images: When you use "Share image", the front of the image shows your player name and the back shows the QR code.
  • Encrypted part: When you create a memento on this device and the device is signed in to iCloud (whether or not "iCloud saves" is on), the QR code also carries encrypted data: your player name and a save backup that can be restored with the same iCloud account. Your player name appears only in the encrypted part, never as plain text in the QR code. Like the backup in Section 4, this save has reduced precision: it does not contain records read from Health or any daily values. It does still contain your exact age, gender, class, appearance, class-change trial records, running totals of HP, XP and honor, your player name and opponents' player names. If the QR code does not have enough capacity, the save backup is left out and only the public fields and the encrypted player name remain; that memento then cannot be used to restore a save. If the device is not signed in to iCloud, the QR code contains only the public part. Only a device that can obtain the memento key from the same iCloud account can decrypt it. Next to the encrypted data, the QR code also carries four unencrypted helper values that anyone who scans it can read: (1) a random salt generated for each card; (2) a one-way hash calculated from that salt and your iCloud account identifier, which tells a scanning device whether it uses the same iCloud account. Because every card has its own random salt, the hash is different on every card and cannot be used to link cards to each other; (3) a key ID, which is only a random name for the slot where the key is stored and cannot decrypt anything on its own; and (4) a flag showing whether the card includes a save backup.
  • Cards created before this version: Mementos created and shared in test versions released before this version still carry the full save from that time in their encrypted part. That includes daily progress calculated from health data (for example, HP earned each day, whether you met your goal, your highest single-day step count, and the dates you met your step goal, met your sleep goal or woke up early). These cards are already wherever you shared them, and we cannot recall or change them. In addition, on cards created in test versions before this version, the plain-text account hash is not salted, so it is the same on all of those cards made with the same account.
  • Share with care: Others cannot decrypt the encrypted save, but it is still a copy of your game data. Only share mementos with people you trust, and do not post cards that carry a save publicly.

7. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything else you choose to include (such as your device model). We use this only to reply and resolve your issue. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it or give it to others. Emails are stored in the email service we use (Microsoft Outlook.com) and kept only as long as needed to handle your request; you can ask us to delete them at any time. Please do not send health data by email.

8. Permissions and how to withdraw them

You can change or withdraw permissions in iOS Settings at any time:

Permission Purpose Where to change it
Health Read steps, sleep, workouts and other data for gameplay and in-game displays (see Section 2.1) Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Sweat & Glory; or Health app > your profile picture > Privacy > Apps
Motion & Fitness Read device step data Settings > Privacy & Security > Motion & Fitness
Camera Scan memento QR codes Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera
Local Network Face-to-face battles Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network
Bluetooth Face-to-face battles Settings > Privacy & Security > Bluetooth
Notifications Daily reports and reminders Settings > Notifications > Sweat & Glory
Game Center Read your player nickname Settings > Game Center

After you withdraw Health access, the Game can no longer read new health data and your character stops gaining new growth. Class affinity and class-change trials need the last 7 days of data, so they may show incomplete data or be unavailable. To protect your privacy, iOS does not tell apps whether you denied read access, so the Game simply shows no data. Levels, stats and medals you have already earned are kept.

9. Children

The Game is rated 13+ and is not directed to children under 13. The Game does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. If the age you enter in the Game is 13 to 17, HP and honor are calculated only from steps taken between 6:00 and 22:00 each day, with a lower daily cap; whether you reach 10,000 steps (which decides that day's XP) is still based on the whole day's steps. Parents or guardians with questions about their child's use of the Game can contact us as described in Section 15.

10. Data retention and deletion

  • On our side: The Game does not send data to us. We only keep emails you choose to send us (see Section 7), and you can ask us to delete them.
  • On your device: Deleting the Game deletes the game data stored on your device. The random device ID kept in the iOS Keychain stays only on this device and does not sync to iCloud. While "iCloud saves" is off, the memento key is also kept only in the Keychain on this device. This Keychain data may remain on the device after you delete the Game.
  • iCloud backup: Can be deleted in the Game (see Section 4).
  • Data in Health: Managed by Apple Health. Deleting the Game does not delete data in Health.
  • Information already sent: Match records kept on other players' devices (see Section 5) and mementos you have shared are outside our control.

11. Security

Health data is protected by iOS, face-to-face battle sessions require encryption, and private memento data is encrypted before it is saved. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

12. Where the Game is offered and where data is located

The Game is not offered in mainland China or Vietnam. The Game does not send your data to us, so no cross-border transfer of data to us takes place through the Game. If you email us, your email is stored and processed by our email provider, Microsoft (Outlook.com), under its privacy statement, and the developer reads and replies to it from mainland China. Services provided by Apple are handled by Apple under its policies.
