Sweat & Glory: Pixel Walk RPG Support

About the Game

Sweat & Glory: Pixel Walk RPG (Chinese title 《汗水荣光》) is a pixel-art fitness RPG. Your real-world steps, workouts and sleep become your character's growth: the more you walk, the stronger your hero gets. Choose from five classes (Rogue, Ranger, Adventurer, Cleric and Knight), customize your character's face and outfits, play practice matches, or battle friends face to face.

  • Languages: Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese
  • Price: Free, no ads; the current version has no in-app purchases

FAQ

1. How do I give the Game access to health data?

When you first start the Game, it asks for access to Apple Health and iPhone step counting. Turn on the items you want to share (steps, sleep and so on). You can check access status under Profile > Health data and access.

After this first setup, the Game can't ask again. Change access in iOS instead:

  • Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Sweat & Glory, then turn on the items you want; or
  • Open the Health app > your profile picture > Privacy > Apps > Sweat & Glory.

In the Game, you can also open the same screen from Profile > ⚙ Settings > Activity > Health access.

The Game only reads health data and never writes to Health. The data is used only on your device to calculate in-game growth and is never uploaded.

2. How do I turn off health data access?

Turn the items off in the same place. After that, your character stops gaining new growth, but levels, stats and medals you've already earned are kept. Class affinity and class-change trials need the last 7 days of data, so they may show incomplete data or be unavailable. iOS doesn't tell apps whether you denied read access, so the Game shows no data.

3. How do steps turn into XP?

Reaching 10,000 steps in a day earns 100 XP. Each consecutive day you reach the goal adds +2%, up to ×1.6. Each day's steps are settled the next time the Game refreshes, and settled days are never recalculated. For players under 18, HP only counts steps taken between 6:00 and 22:00. For how other data (workouts, sleep) affects your character, see the in-game help.

4. Do steps I add manually in the Health app count?

No. The Game only counts steps recorded by devices such as iPhone or Apple Watch. Steps and workouts added manually in the Health app are not counted.

5. My steps aren't syncing or show 0

  1. Open the Health app and check that today's steps recorded by your devices are there. If Health has no steps either, the issue is with the data source, not the Game.
  2. Check permissions: make sure Steps and the other items are on for Sweat & Glory (see question 1). Also check Settings > Privacy & Security > Motion & Fitness, and make sure Fitness Tracking and the Game are on. If Health has no steps for the day, the Game fills in with iPhone step counting.
  3. If you use Apple Watch, it can take a while for watch data to sync to iPhone. In the Health app, go to your profile picture > Devices > your watch > Privacy Settings and make sure Fitness Tracking is on.
  4. Unlock your iPhone before opening the Game. By design, apps can't read health data while the iPhone is locked.
  5. The Game refreshes automatically when it starts, when it returns to the foreground and when you come back from the Health data screen. You can also tap the refresh button on the Home screen, or "Refresh data" on the Health data screen.
  6. Days are split at midnight in your device's current time zone. If you travel across time zones, the day's number may look different than expected.

6. Moving to a new phone: what does iCloud save?

  • iCloud backup is off by default. First turn on "iCloud saves" in Profile > ⚙ Settings > Backup (you must be signed in to iCloud). You can tap "Back up now".
  • On the new phone, sign in to the same Apple Account, go to Profile > ⚙ Settings > Backup > "Manage backups and restore", choose a backup and tap "Restore this backup".
  • iCloud sync can take a little time. Stay online after saving and make sure the backup appears in the list before you switch phones.
  • Backups contain game progress only, not records read from Health. See Section 4 of the Privacy Policy for details. After a restore, your streak of goal days starts again from zero. You'll need to allow Health access again on the new phone.
  • Backups are stored in your own iCloud account. We can't access or recover them.
  • You can also restore with a memento: in Profile > Achievements > "Moments to Keep", open a card and tap "Back" to show its QR code. On the new phone, go to Profile > ⚙ Settings > Backup > "Restore from QR" and scan it. Conditions for restoring:
    • Both phones use the same iCloud account, and the old phone was signed in to iCloud when the card was created. The new phone does not need "iCloud saves" turned on.
    • "iCloud saves" must have been turned on on the old phone at some point. If it was always off, the decryption key stays only on the old phone, and scanning on the new phone shows that the restore was not completed. Exceptions: you turned the switch on after creating the card (the current key is then uploaded), or the card was created in test build 26 or earlier.
    • Mementos restored from an iCloud backup can no longer be used to restore a save. Restoring a backup replaces the whole card collection, so after the old phone itself restores an iCloud backup, its cards can no longer restore a save either.
    • Scanning also cannot restore a save if the card has no save snapshot (for example, the save was too large, so the QR code keeps only the public part; the back of the card says "No restore snapshot is included"), or if your iCloud already has a backup newer than the card. In that case, restore the newer backup from "Manage backups and restore" instead.
  • You can delete backups you no longer need in "Manage backups and restore" with "Delete backup".

7. I played a development build or a TestFlight beta. Can I bring my save to the App Store version?

Cards and backups from development builds can't be moved to the App Store version. TestFlight beta builds and the App Store version share saves (with the same Apple Account).

8. Do I have to sign in to Game Center?

No. You can play fully without signing in. Your player name will appear as "Player" followed by 4 digits.

9. I can't connect for a face-to-face battle

  1. Update both devices to the latest version. Devices with different versions or battle rules can't match.
  2. Turn on Wi-Fi on both devices. You don't need to join the same Wi-Fi network or be online, but Bluetooth alone with Wi-Fi off won't work.
  3. Turn on Sweat & Glory in Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network. The Local Network prompt appears the first time you open the Battle tab. If you did not allow it then, the Game shows "Allow local network access for Sweat & Glory in Settings."
  4. Keep the devices close together, with the Game in the foreground and the screen on, on both devices. Switching away disconnects you, and leaving the Game before a battle starts cancels it.
  5. If it still fails, both players leave the room, fully close the Game, reopen it and try again.

Note: only while the Battle tab is in the foreground, nearby devices can see your player name (as the connection name); in lobby and nearby modes, also the battle mode, a random seat ID and your class (shown to them as "Class shared by this player"). Your level, honor, medals, device name and health data are never included. Battle data after connecting is encrypted. Only battle people you have confirmed in person.

10. Does the Game support iPhone Duo?

The launch version includes three iPhone Duo adjustments:

  • Buttons stay clear of the screen fold;
  • Battles and the intro video play in landscape;
  • The duplicate Shop and Start Battle buttons are removed from the home screen.

Any future changes will be listed in the App Store release notes.

11. Are there ads or paid content?

No ads. The current version has no in-app purchases.

12. How do I change the language?

The Game supports Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English and Japanese. It follows your system language by default ("Follow system"), and you can switch in Profile > ⚙ Settings > General > Language.

13. How do I delete my data?

The Game doesn't send data to us. You can delete the Game (which removes its data from your device), delete iCloud backups in "Manage backups and restore", and withdraw permissions in iOS Settings. If you have emailed us, you can ask us to delete your emails. See the Privacy Policy for details.
